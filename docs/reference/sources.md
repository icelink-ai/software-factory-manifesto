## How to weigh them

- S3 is a hands-on tutorial and S6 is a product demo. Use them for mechanics, not as evidence of
  results at scale.
- The headline numbers in S1, S2 and S9 (PR volume, revert rate) were reported by the speakers
  after six to twelve months of investment in their environments. Treat them as direction, not
  as targets.
- Where sources disagree, the page says so and states the position this manifesto takes. The
  main disagreements are collected in [Anti-patterns and disagreements](../09-adoption/anti-patterns.md).
- `R` marks a practice that comes from running our own factory rather than from a talk.
